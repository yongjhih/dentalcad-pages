# Surface brief: Dental OrthoCAD 3D & Case Viewer

Scope: Single-page CAD viewer and case record surface for iTero digital dental scans.
Visitor mode: Operate / Experience
Audience: Orthodontists, dental specialists, lab technicians, and case reviewers.
Job: Inspect 3D dental STL models in occlusion, evaluate clinical dental arches, examine 5-view photography, and review digital prescription order details.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
Orthodontic CAD software shouldn't require cumbersome desktop software installs or slow cloud queues for primary scan review. This interface delivers a clinical-grade, browser-based 3D occlusion viewer that treats real dental STL files as first-class interactive data, paired with authentic penta-view photography and prescription metadata. It refuses the generic startup hero template and generic 3D file drop-zone aesthetic.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep slate obsidian (`#0b0f17`, `#111827`, `#1e293b`) with clinical cyan and enamel ivory accents (`#38bdf8`, `#f8fafc`). Surgical clarity, high-contrast crisp labels, tabular numerals for 3D coordinates and vertex counts, and tactile medical toolbar switches.

### STORY
The practitioner or reviewer opens the case, immediately sees the dual-arch 3D digital model loaded in true occlusion, toggles upper/lower views or separates the arches to check contacts, cross-references with the penta-view photographic records, and verifies patient and prescription specifications.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
An integrated dual-pane CAD workspace: left viewport dominates with the interactive Three.js 3D Dental Canvas, overlaid with viewport orientation cube / camera preset pill controls and real-time mesh statistics; right pane provides tabs for Case Dossier (Order, Patient, Clinic), Penta Gallery (5-view clinical photography lightbox), Shading & Occlusion Controls, and Custom STL Import.

### FORM
Clinical Medical CAD Suite, form position 1. Seed: none (unavailable).

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
